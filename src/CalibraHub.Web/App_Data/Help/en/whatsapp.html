The WhatsApp screen lets you have real-time, one-on-one WhatsApp conversations with your customers and contacts. The interface is similar to WhatsApp Web: the chat list on the left, the message panel of the selected chat on the right.

Chat List

  • Chats are listed with the last message, unread count, and the contact/account name.
  • Narrow the list with the All / Unread / Groups filters; you can search from the top.
  • If a number is linked to an account, the account title appears in the chat header.

Messaging

  • Send: Text, media (image/video/document), and voice messages (push-to-talk).
  • Reply (quoted), Forward, Reactions (emoji), message deletion.
  • Read/delivered status (check marks), "typing…", and online/last seen status are updated in real time.
  • In-chat search, clearing/deleting a chat, marking as read/unread.

Groups and Account Linking

  • Group chats are supported; group members and subject information are shown.
  • If a contact matches the WhatsApp number on an account card, the chat is linked to that account; you can also jump straight into the chat from the account card.

Connection Status

The status bar at the top shows the connection (ready / connecting / waiting for QR, etc.). During initial setup, pairing with your phone via a QR code may be required.

Note: This screen is for one-on-one conversations (not bulk sending). WhatsApp connection/provider settings are configured on the WhatsApp tab under Company Settings.
