The Traceability tab defines the uniqueness rule applied when serial numbers are entered.

First: Where Is Tracking Enabled?

Lot/serial tracking is enabled per material: Item Card → Traceability. The parameter here defines the scope of the check for materials that are tracked.

Serial Number Uniqueness

  • Per Item: The same serial number may be used on different materials; it is unique only within one material. Suitable when manufacturer serial schemes can collide.
  • Global (barcode-like): A serial number is unique across the whole company; once used it cannot be reused on another item card. This gives the strictest traceability.

Things to Watch

  • Moving from Global to Per Item relaxes the rule; moving the other way may reveal conflicting serials in existing data.
  • The rule applies to new entries only; recorded serials are not changed.

Note: Whether serials are entered at order stage is on the Sales Order tab, and whether they may be swapped at shipment is on the Stock tab.
