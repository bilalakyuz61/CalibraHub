The Sales Order screen lists customer orders in a card view. An order is a confirmed sales request waiting to be delivered.

Cards

  • The card shows the document number, account, total amount, status, delivery/fulfillment status, and date.
  • Card actions: Edit, Copy, Delete, audit log.

Flow

An order is delivered by converting it to a Sales Delivery Note (partial delivery is supported). To commit stock in advance, a reservation can be made from the Shipment Planning screen.

Note: The order itself does not deduct stock; the stock effect occurs at the delivery note/shipment stage.
