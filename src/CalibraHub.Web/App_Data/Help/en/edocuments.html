The Electronic Documents screen lets you view, approve and convert incoming e-Invoice, e-Archive and e-Dispatch documents into CalibraHub documents.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Incoming documents are listed; lines, amounts and taxes can be reviewed.
  • An approved document can be imported into CalibraHub as a purchase dispatch/invoice.
  • The Source field shows whether the document came from the online integrator or an offline import.

Note: integrator settings are defined under System Settings; this screen only processes incoming documents.
