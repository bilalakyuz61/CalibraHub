The Asset Management screen is the card board where you track assets such as equipment, machines, measuring devices, vehicles and computers in one place. Assignment, maintenance and calibration processes are managed from here.

Tabs

  • Assets: All asset records (type, status, owner, location).
  • Assignment: Who each asset is assigned to; assign/return operations.
  • Calibration Tracking: Devices requiring calibration and their next dates.
  • Maintenance Tracking: Maintenance plan/history and upcoming maintenance.

Cards and Actions

  • The card shows the asset name, type, status and the related date/person.
  • New Asset: Opens a blank asset card.
  • From the card: Edit, Assign, Perform Maintenance, Perform Calibration.

Note: Production machines can also be tracked as assets; an asset record is created automatically from the machine card on the first operation (unified inventory).
