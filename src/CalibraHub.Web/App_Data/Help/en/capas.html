The CAPA screen lists Corrective and Preventive Actions in a card view. A CAPA is an improvement record opened to eliminate the root cause of a nonconformity and prevent its recurrence.

What Is a CAPA?

It is opened upon a nonconformity, a customer complaint or an internal finding. Its source can be an inspection, a purchase delivery note or an account. It is closed with root cause analysis, an action plan and verification.

Cards and Actions

  • The card shows the CAPA number, source, owner, status and date.
  • New / Edit: Opens the CAPA record (actions, owner, status).
  • Delete: Removes the CAPA.

Note: A CAPA can also be started directly from the "Open CAPA" button on the related inspection/document screen; the source information is carried over automatically.
