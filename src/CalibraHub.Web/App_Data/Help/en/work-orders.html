The Production Work Orders screen lists the work orders opened to produce your finished products in specific quantities, in a card view. Each card represents one work order; you see the status, quantity, and due date at a glance.

What Is a Work Order?

A work order is an order opened to produce a finished product. Its BOM (component list) and routing (operation steps) are exploded; as it progresses on the shop floor, components are deducted from stock, and when completed the product is received into stock.

Top Toolbar

  • Search: Search by order number, product code/name.
  • Filter: Filter by fields such as status, priority, responsible person.
  • Excel: Exports the visible work orders.
  • Widget settings: Determines which details are shown on the cards.
  • New Work Order: Opens a blank work order edit screen.

Cards

  • The title shows the product name, with the order number (and revision) below it.
  • Info badges: Status, Planned and Produced quantity, Priority, Date, Planned End (red if the due date has passed = delayed), Responsible.
  • Click the card to edit.
  • To cancel, use the "Cancel" action on the card (confirmation required).

Statuses

  • Planned — newly opened, not yet in production.
  • Released — routing exploded, ready for production.
  • In Progress — production has started on the shop floor.
  • Completed — the planned quantity has been produced.
  • Closed — permanently closed (no further transactions allowed).
  • Canceled — canceled.

Note: Canceled and Closed orders are hidden in the default view; select the corresponding status filter to see them.
