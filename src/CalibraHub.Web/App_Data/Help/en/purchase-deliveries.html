The Purchase Delivery Note screen lists the goods receipt documents you have received from suppliers. A delivery note represents the actual entry of goods into the warehouse.

Cards

  • The card shows the document no, supplier, amount, status, and date.
  • Card actions: Edit, Copy, Delete, audit log.

Stock Impact

A purchase delivery note creates a stock receipt — the received materials are added to the warehouse. It is usually created by converting a purchase order.

Note: For lot/serial-tracked materials, the received lot/serial is specified on the document.
