The BOM screen lists the bills of materials of your finished products in a card view. A BOM defines the components and quantities required to produce a finished product.

What Is a BOM?

Each card shows the bill of materials of a finished product (or a variant/combination). When a work order is opened, the BOM is "exploded" to create the component requirements and lower-level semi-finished products.

Cards and Actions

  • The card shows the finished product, the component count and the status.
  • New / Edit: Opens the BOM edit screen.
  • Delete: Removes the BOM.

Note: In multi-level production, a product's BOM contains the BOMs of its lower-level semi-finished products; this is how the tree gains depth.
