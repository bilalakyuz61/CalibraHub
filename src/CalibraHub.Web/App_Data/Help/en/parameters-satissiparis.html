The Sales Order tab defines how orders affect stock and how serial numbers behave. The three parameters are hierarchical: turning off the upper one effectively disables the ones below it.

1) Sales Order Affects Stock (Reservation)

When enabled, saving an order creates a logical reservation: the quantity does not physically leave the warehouse but cannot be allocated to another document. When disabled, the order is only a commitment and does not touch stock.

2) Serial Tracking on Orders

Available only while stock reservation is on. When enabled, a "Serial" column appears on order lines so you can record which serials belong to the order.

3) Order Serial Reservation

Available only while serial tracking on orders is on. Entered serials are reserved for that order and cannot be shipped on another document.

Note: When an upper level is switched off, the lower ones are shown as inactive — if you cannot find a setting, first make sure the parameter above it is enabled. Whether serials may be swapped at shipment is on the Stock tab.
