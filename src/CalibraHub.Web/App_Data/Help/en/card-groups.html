The Card Groups screen defines the 5-level hierarchical group tree used to classify material and account cards.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Groups are defined level by level (Group 1 → Group 5), nested within each other.
  • The "Groups" tab of the material card and the "Groups" tab of the account card are fed from this tree.
  • Enables filtering by group in reporting and searches.

Actions

  • New / Edit: Manages the group record.
  • Delete: Removes the group (mind its subgroups and linked cards).
