The R&D / Product Development Projects screen shows your research & development and new product development projects on a "command deck" board grouped by lifecycle stage.

What Is This Screen For?

Each card represents a project; projects are grouped by their stage (e.g. idea, design, prototype, transition to production). The counters at the top summarize the distribution of active / in-production / at-risk projects.

Cards and Actions

  • The card shows the project name, stage, owner and status.
  • New Project: Opens a blank project edit screen.
  • Edit: Click the card to open the project (prototype, task and cost tabs).
  • Status/stage changes can be made directly on the card.

Note: Project cost is aggregated (rollup) from warehouse issues, prototype BOM/routing and linked work orders, and is tracked on the project screen.
