The Purchase Order screen lists the purchase orders you have placed with suppliers. An order is a finalized purchase commitment awaiting receipt.

Cards

  • The card shows the document no, supplier, amount, status, and date.
  • Card actions: Edit, Copy, Delete, audit log.

Flow

Order → when the goods arrive, they are received by converting the order into a Purchase Delivery Note (partial receipt is supported). The order itself does not increase stock; the stock impact occurs on the delivery note.
