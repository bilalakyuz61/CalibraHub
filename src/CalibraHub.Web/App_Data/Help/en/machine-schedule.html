The Machine Scheduling screen lets you plan work order operations onto machines on a timeline (Gantt).

What Is This Screen For?

  • Rows show the machines, blocks show the scheduled operations.
  • You can change the time/machine by dragging the blocks; durations come from the operations' standard times.
  • Auto-scheduling places pending jobs forward according to capacity; a preview is shown first, and it is applied once you confirm.
  • Hours outside the work calendar and holidays appear shaded; setup blocks are added automatically.

Note: Machine working hours and holidays are managed on the "Machine Work Calendar" screen.
