The Purchase Demands screen lists requests coming from departments and forms the first step of the procurement chain.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Demands can be filtered by status, date and requester.
  • An approved demand can be converted into an RFQ or directly into a purchase order.
  • The fulfilled/remaining quantity of a demand is tracked along the chain.

Note: if an approval flow is defined, a demand cannot be converted into an order before the flow completes.
