The Purchase Demands screen lists the purchase demands created for procurement from suppliers. It is the formal purchasing step that follows the Purchase Request.

Cards

  • The card shows the document no, supplier, amount, status, and date.
  • Card actions: Edit, Copy, Delete, and if needed Send for Approval, audit log.

New Demand

The New button at the top opens the wizard that creates the demand step by step. Afterwards the demand can be converted into a Purchase Quote/Order.
