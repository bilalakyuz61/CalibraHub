The Department Definitions screen lets you define your company's departments (e.g. Production, Purchasing, Quality, Accounting).

What Is This Screen For?

Departments are used for personnel, work order responsibility, approval flows, and classification in reporting.

Cards and Actions

  • New / Edit: Manages department details.
  • Delete: Removes the department.

Note: You do not enter a code for a department; uniqueness is enforced by name.
