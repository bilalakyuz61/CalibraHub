The Sales Delivery Note screen lists shipment (delivery) documents issued to customers. A delivery note represents the physical issue and delivery of goods.

Cards

  • The card shows the document number, account, amount, status, and date.
  • Card actions: Edit, Copy, Delete, audit log.

Stock Effect

A sales delivery note creates a stock issue — the shipped materials are deducted from the warehouse. It is usually created by converting a sales order (partial delivery is possible).

Note: For lot/serial-tracked materials, the shipped lot/serial is specified on the document.
