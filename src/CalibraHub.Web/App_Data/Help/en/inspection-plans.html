The Inspection Plans screen defines control templates for materials or material groups. A plan determines what will be measured during an inspection and within which tolerances it will be accepted.

What Is an Inspection Plan?

A plan is a template containing the control characteristics (dimensional, visual, qualitative) and tolerances (lower/upper limits) for a material (or its group) and an inspection type. When an inspection is opened, this plan is applied automatically.

Cards and Actions

  • The card shows the plan name, the material/group it covers and the number of characteristics.
  • New / Edit: Edits the plan's characteristics and tolerances.
  • Delete: Removes the plan.

Note: A plan defined for a material group applies to all materials in that group; if a material-specific plan is defined, it overrides the group plan.
