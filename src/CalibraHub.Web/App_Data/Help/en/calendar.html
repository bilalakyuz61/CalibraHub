The Calendar screen brings together your personal events, work orders and birthdays in a single view. You can browse by month or week and see the details of the selected day on the timeline in the right panel.

Event Sources

The source badges in the left panel determine what appears on the calendar. Click a source to hide it and show it again; the number next to it is the event count for that period.

  • Personal — Events you created. Editable and deletable.
  • Work Orders — Planned production work orders. Read-only; cannot be changed from here.
  • Birthdays — Employee birthdays. Read-only.

Views

  • Month: Shows the whole month as a grid. Each cell contains the event strips for that day; those that do not fit are collapsed as "+N more".
  • Week: Shows a single week. The ISO week number is shown on the left.

Switch views with the Month / Week buttons at the top right. Use the Today button to return to today.

Navigation

  • Previous/next period with the ‹ › arrows in the header.
  • Clicking the period label (e.g. "August 2026") opens the year/month picker.
  • You can also change the period with the mouse wheel over the calendar.
  • When you click a day, the day panel on the right shows that day's hourly timeline (07:00–21:00 plus all-day events).

Adding / Editing Personal Events

  • New event: Click the "New event" button in the right day panel; in the dialog that opens, enter the title, date/time and note, then save.
  • Editing: Click a personal event and update its details.
  • Deleting: Remove it with the delete button in the event dialog.
  • Work order and birthday events are read-only; they are managed at their source.

Keyboard Shortcuts

← →Previous / next day (or period)
↑ ↓Navigate within the period
TGo to today
MMonth view
WWeek view

Note: While typing in a text box or while a dialog is open, the letter shortcuts (T, M, W) are disabled.

Tips

  • On busy days, click the "+N more" label to see all events in the day panel.
  • To focus only on your own events, hide the "Work Orders" and "Birthdays" sources in the left panel.
