The Fulfillment Center is the screen where you fulfill open purchase requests in bulk. You can close each request line from the warehouse, via transfer, or through purchasing.

What Is This Screen For?

It lists the open (unfulfilled) request lines and shows the stock balance. You select one or more lines and apply the appropriate fulfillment action.

Fulfillment Actions

  • Fulfill from Warehouse: Fulfills from existing stock (warehouse issue/consumption).
  • Warehouse Transfer: Fulfills via a transfer from another warehouse.
  • Purchase: Creates a purchase demand or a draft purchase order.
  • Close Request: Closes the line as fulfilled.

Note: If the approval flow is enabled, only approved requests can be fulfilled. As fulfillment progresses, the status of the related purchase request is updated.
