The Sales Representatives screen defines your sales representatives in a card view. Representatives are assigned to accounts and documents and used for sales performance tracking.

Cards and Actions

  • The card shows the representative's name and status.
  • New / Edit: Edits the representative's details.
  • Delete: Deactivates/removes the representative.

Note: The "Sales Representative" field on an account record is selected from this list; this allows sales documents to be reported by representative.
