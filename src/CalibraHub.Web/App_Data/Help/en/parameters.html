The Company Parameters screen is where you tailor the program's behavior to your company by turning business rules on and off.

What Can Be Configured?

  • Stock / Traceability: Stock impact, negative balance control, lot/serial tracking, FIFO delivery.
  • Approval / e-Document: Which document types require an approval flow.
  • Production: ShopFloor (production terminal) PIN rules.
  • Security: Settings such as session timeout and audit log retention period.

Note: Parameters are set with on/off switches and applied with Save. Changing a parameter affects new transactions; historical records are preserved.
