The MRP (Material Requirements Planning) screen starts from sales orders, calculates what has to be produced and in what quantity, and creates the work orders.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Open order lines are scanned; available stock and open work orders are deducted.
  • The remaining requirement is exploded multi-level through the bill of materials, so sub-assemblies also appear in the list.
  • You select from the preview and create the work orders in bulk.

Things To Watch

  • To avoid converting the same requirement twice, a created work order is pegged to the order line; that quantity is deducted on the next run.
  • How a product's work orders are split comes from the split policy on the item card.
  • No work order is suggested for a product without a BOM; define the bill of materials first.
