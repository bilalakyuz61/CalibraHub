The Electronic Documents screen lists and displays incoming e-Invoice, e-Archive, and e-Delivery Note documents and manages their approval/rejection.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Electronic documents received from the integrator are listed by type and date.
  • Clicking a document displays its contents (lines, amounts, sender).
  • If the approval parameter is enabled, the document can be approved / rejected; if an approval flow is defined, it is routed to the relevant people.

Note: The integrator connection details are configured in Company Settings.
