The Price List Edit screen lets you enter and edit material prices for a price group in bulk on a grid.

What Is This Screen For?

  • You enter unit prices by material × currency breakdown.
  • Prices are kept per date/validity and per currency.
  • Entered prices are filled in automatically on the documents of accounts linked to the relevant price group.

Usage

  • Select the price group at the top, list the materials, enter the prices, and click Save.
  • Bulk paste from Excel and quick row entry may be supported.
