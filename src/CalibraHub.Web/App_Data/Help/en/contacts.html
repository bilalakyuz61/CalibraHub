The Accounts screen lists all your accounts — customers, suppliers, or both — in a card view. From here you can create new accounts, search, filter, and open cards for editing.

What Is an Account?

An account is the entity that documents (orders, delivery notes, invoices, etc.) are linked to. Its type can be Customer, Supplier, or Both. Tax/ID information, price group, and contact details are stored on the account card; the balance is calculated from the documents belonging to that account.

Top Toolbar

  • Search: Search by code, title, or tax number.
  • Filter: Filter by fields such as type (Customer/Supplier), province, district, tax number.
  • Excel: Exports the visible accounts with their columns.
  • Widget settings: Determines which details appear on the cards and in what order.
  • New Account: Opens a blank account editing screen.

Cards

  • The header shows the title, below it the code, and on the third line the province / district.
  • Info badges: Phone, E-Mail, Tax No, Province, District, and Type (Customer = green, Supplier = purple).
  • Click a card to edit it.
  • Use the delete action on the card to delete it (confirmation is required).
  • Cards may also include integration actions such as Audit Log and (if you are authorized) Send to ERP.

Tips

  • To focus only on customers or only on suppliers, select the Type in the Filter.
  • With many records, narrow down with search first; the list loads in pages.
