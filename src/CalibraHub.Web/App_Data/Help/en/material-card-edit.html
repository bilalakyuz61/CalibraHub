The Material Card Edit screen lets you manage all the details of a material in one place. Section links are on the left, the fields of the selected section on the right, and the fixed Back / Save (and, for an existing card, Delete) bar at the top. Sections appear/hide depending on the material type and settings.

General Information

  • Material Code and Material Name (required), Barcode.
  • Material Type: Raw Material, Semi-Finished, Finished Good, Trade Good, Consumable or Kit. The selected type determines the visibility of sections below such as BOM / Kit.
  • Base Unit (required) and VAT Rate %.
  • Combination Tracking: When enabled, the Attributes and Combinations sections appear (variant management such as color/size).
  • Traceability: Lot and/or Serial tracking; with Serial tracking, the "Auto Receipt Serial" option.
  • The Overall Minimum Stock and the Document Locks summary are also in this section.

Units of Measure

Alternative units besides the base unit and their conversion factors (such as "1 box = 12 pieces"). Stock is always kept normalized to the base unit.

Groups

5-level hierarchical grouping (Group 1 → Group 5). Used to classify materials in reporting and filtering.

Planning

Location-based planning details — which warehouses/locations the material will be kept in and the related planning parameters.

Stock Movements

The material's receipt/issue movement statement: running balance, period summary, date / movement type / location filters and Excel export. Movements are fed from document lines (read-only view).

Prices

Purchase/sales price entry by price list, currency and validity date, plus the price history. In document entry, the line price comes automatically from these definitions.

Sections Depending on Type / Settings

  • Attributes & Combinations: Visible when combination tracking is enabled (variant attribute mapping and combination generation).
  • BOM: Bill of materials definition for Finished / Semi-Finished types.
  • Kit Contents: For the Kit type, the components that make up the bundle.
  • Custom Fields: Custom fields defined for this form in "Field Management" are added automatically as separate section(s).

Saving

  • The Save button at the top saves all sections together.
  • Back returns to the list without saving.
  • On an existing card, Delete is visible; a centered confirmation dialog appears before deleting.
  • If required fields (Name, Base Unit) are empty, saving is blocked and the field is highlighted.

Note: The change history can be viewed in the "Change History" section of the related record.
