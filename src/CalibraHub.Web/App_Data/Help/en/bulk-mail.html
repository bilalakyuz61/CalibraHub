The Bulk Mail screen lets you send (personalized) e-mail to your account contacts in bulk, and lists your past sends in a card view.

What Is This Screen For?

Each card is a bulk send (batch) job. The card shows the subject, the template used, the Total / Sent / Failed counters, the date and the sender. Click a card to see the per-recipient send status (who it went to, what the error was).

Sending

  • New Send at the top opens the 3-step bulk send wizard (template & target → mail body → recipient list).
  • From a record, Detail shows that send's results, Delete removes the record.

Prerequisites

  • SMTP profile: An active e-mail account must be defined under Administration → Company Settings → SMTP for sending.
  • Mail template: The design to be used must be marked with "Also use as mail template" in the Document Designer.

Note: The send status is kept separately for each recipient; the error message of failed ones is shown on the Detail screen. There is no scheduling/quota, sending happens immediately.
