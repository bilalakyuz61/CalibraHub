The Asset Edit screen lets you manage all information and the lifecycle history of an asset (equipment/machine/device etc.).

General Info

  • Asset name, type (equipment, machine, measuring device, vehicle, computer…), serial/tag number, category.
  • Location, responsible department and status (active / faulty / scrapped etc.).
  • Purchase, warranty and, if any, value/depreciation details.

History / Details

  • Assignment history: Who the asset was assigned to and when.
  • Maintenance / calibration history: Performed operations, results and next dates.

Saving

Save stores the asset; Back returns to the list without saving.

Note: Assignment and maintenance/calibration operations are performed on their own transaction screens; here they are viewed as history.
