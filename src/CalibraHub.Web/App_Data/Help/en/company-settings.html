The Company Settings screen lets you manage your company's basic information and system integration settings on a single page, organized in tabs. All tabs are stored with a single "Save".

Tabs

  • General: Company name, tax details, logo and basic identity information.
  • Mail / SMTP: Server (host/port/SSL), sender address and credentials for e-mail sending.
  • Integrator: e-Invoice/e-Document integrator connection details.
  • Addresses: Company address(es).
  • WhatsApp: WhatsApp connection/notification settings.
  • Artificial Intelligence: AI assistant provider settings.

Note: The SMTP setting on the Mail tab is required for Bulk Mail and approval notifications. Changes are applied together with Save at the bottom of the page.
