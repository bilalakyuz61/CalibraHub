The Assignment screen lets you assign an asset to an employee (or a department) or take it back as a return.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Assign: Hands the asset over to the relevant person; the handover date and a note are entered.
  • Return: Takes back an assigned asset; the asset becomes available for assignment again.

Handover Form

After the assignment, the Assignment Handover Form can be printed and kept as a signed document.

Note: The assignment history is tracked from the asset card and the Assignment tab.
