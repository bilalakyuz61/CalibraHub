The Organization Chart screen lets you define and view your company's hierarchy (who reports to whom) as a visual tree.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Personnel/users are positioned in the tree based on their reporting relationships.
  • You can change reporting lines by editing the nodes.

Note: The "Requester's manager" approver type in approval flows finds the manager from this chart — keeping the chart up to date is important so approvals reach the right person.
