The Warehouse Receipt (Stock In) screen lists documents that bring stock into the warehouse. Non-purchase receipts (production receipts, count surplus, returns, opening stock, etc.) are managed here.

What Is This Screen For?

Each card is a warehouse receipt document. A receipt document adds material to the selected location (stock increase). The card shows the document number, location, line count/amount, and date.

Toolbar and Cards

  • New Receipt: Opens the edit screen where you enter the location and line items (material, quantity, unit).
  • Card actions: Edit, Delete, audit log.

Note: For lot/serial-tracked materials, lot/serial details are requested during receipt. For purchase receipts (from suppliers), use the "Purchase Delivery Note" document.
