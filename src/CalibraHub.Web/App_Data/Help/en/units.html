The Units of Measure screen defines the units of measure (piece, kg, m, liter, box, etc.) used on materials and documents.

Cards and Actions

  • Each card shows one unit of measure.
  • New / Edit: Edits the unit details.
  • Delete: Removes the unit.

Note: Conversion factors between a material's base unit and its alternative units are defined on the "Units of Measure" tab of the material card.
