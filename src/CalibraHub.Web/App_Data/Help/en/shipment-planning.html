The Shipment Planning Center is where you plan shipments from open sales order lines and reserve stock. It manages which order will be fulfilled when, and with which stock.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Lists open (undelivered) order lines.
  • Makes stock reservations for the selected lines (logical allocation) — the stock is not physically deducted; it is blocked so other orders cannot take it.
  • Planned items are delivered by converting them to a delivery note/shipment.
  • Reservation and loading are performed from the Actions menu at the start of the row.

Note: Reservation is the sales-side counterpart of Fulfillment; you see stock availability and set priorities.
