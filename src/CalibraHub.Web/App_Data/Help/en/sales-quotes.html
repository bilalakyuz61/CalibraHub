The Sales Quote screen lists the sales quotes you have given to customers in a card view. You create new quotes, edit or copy existing ones, and convert them to orders.

Cards

  • The card shows the document number, account (customer), total amount, status, and date.
  • Card actions: Edit, Copy, Delete, and where applicable Send for Approval and the audit log.

Flow

A quote is prepared → (goes through approval if required) → when the customer accepts, it is converted to a Sales Order. A quote does not create any stock movement; it is only a price/terms offer.

Note: On the quote edit screen you enter the account, line items (material, quantity, price), and terms; prices can be filled automatically from the price list.
