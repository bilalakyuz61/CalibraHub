The Transfer screen lists stock transfer documents between warehouses or locations. You create a transfer document to move a material from one warehouse/location to another.

What Is This Screen For?

Each card is a transfer document (source → target location, line items, date, status). A transfer creates an issue effect at the source location + a receipt effect at the target location; the total stock does not change, only its location does.

Toolbar and Cards

  • New Transfer: Opens the edit screen where you select the source/target and enter the line items.
  • The card shows the document number, source/target, amount/line count, and date.
  • Card actions: Edit, Delete, audit log.

Note: If negative-balance control is enabled, the transfer may be blocked when there is not enough stock at the source location.
