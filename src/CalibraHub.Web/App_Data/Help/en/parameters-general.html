The General tab holds parameters that affect the whole company and do not belong to a single module.

E-Document Approval System

Determines whether incoming e-Invoice and e-Archive documents are processed directly or must first pass a human approval.

  • Active: Incoming e-documents enter the Approval Tasks flow. The document is not posted to the ERP until the assigned approver accepts or rejects it.
  • Passive: Documents are treated as accepted immediately; no approval step is required.

When Should It Be Enabled?

  • When supplier invoices must be checked by purchasing before reaching accounting.
  • When you want to catch amount/quantity mismatches before the document is posted.

Note: The change is applied with Save and affects only documents received afterwards; documents already awaiting approval stay in their flow.
