The Maintenance / Calibration screen lets you enter a maintenance or calibration record for an asset. Depending on where it was opened from, the screen comes up in Maintenance or Calibration mode.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Operation date and the person/company that performed it.
  • Result: Work performed for maintenance; measurement/conformity result for calibration.
  • Next date: The next maintenance/calibration plan — a reminder appears on the tracking screen.
  • A certificate/report can be attached if available.

Note: The saved operation is written to the asset's history and is tracked as upcoming dates on the "Maintenance Tracking" / "Calibration Tracking" tabs.
