The CAPA Dashboard summarizes the overall status of Corrective/Preventive Actions with KPI cards and charts.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Indicators such as the number of open / overdue / closed CAPAs and the average closure time.
  • Distributions by source, type, and responsible person.
  • You can navigate from an indicator to the CAPA list.

Note: The CAPA records themselves are managed from the "CAPA (Corrective/Preventive Action)" screen; this dashboard is for monitoring only.
