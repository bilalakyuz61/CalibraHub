The Purchasing tab controls whether purchasing documents enter the approval workflow. Each document type is independent: you can enable one and leave the other off.

1) Purchase Quote Approval

When enabled, a saved purchase quote enters the defined approval flow and cannot proceed until approved. When disabled, the quote is usable right away.

2) Purchase Order Approval

When enabled, the purchase order enters the approval flow; until approval is complete it stays pending for later steps such as goods receipt.

Note: Enabling the parameter alone is not enough — an active flow must exist for that document type under Approval Flow Definitions. Without a defined flow the document proceeds without approval. Sales-side approval parameters live on the Sales Quote and Sales Order tabs; purchase request approval is on the Purchase Requests tab.
