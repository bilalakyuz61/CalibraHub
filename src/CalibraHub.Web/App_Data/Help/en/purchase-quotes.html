The Purchase Quote screen lists the price quotes you have received from suppliers. You compare the quotes and convert the suitable one into an order.

Cards

  • The card shows the document no, supplier, amount, status, and date.
  • Card actions: Edit, Copy, Delete, audit log.

Flow

A quote is requested from the demand/request → the supplier's price is entered → the suitable quote is converted into a Purchase Order. A quote does not create any stock movement.
