The Shop Floor terminal is the tablet/kiosk screen where operators start and complete work order operations.

What Is This Screen For?

  • The operator enters an employee number + PIN or scans an NFC card.
  • An operation is started, paused, and completed by reporting good/scrap quantities.
  • Downtime can be recorded; duration and reason feed the reports.

Things To Watch

  • Repeated wrong PIN attempts temporarily lock the person out.
  • The employee number is shown on the personnel card under the PIN field (0001, 0002…).
  • Keep the PIN prompt on for shared tablets; on a personal phone you can turn off "Ask for PIN on mobile" on the personnel card.
