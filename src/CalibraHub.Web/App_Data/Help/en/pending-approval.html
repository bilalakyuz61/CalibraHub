The Pending Approvals screen is where you view, approve, or reject the documents and steps awaiting your approval. It is the runtime counterpart of the Approval Flow Definitions — work routed to you as a result of the defined flows is listed here.

Views and Modes

  • Pending / Completed: Use the tabs at the top to switch between items awaiting your approval and historical records (approved by you / rejected).
  • Scope: Depending on your permission, the Only Mine / My Department / All options are shown (without permission, only your own items are displayed).
  • Left panel: Groups items by document type; the pending count is shown per group.

List

  • Columns: Document No, Flow, Type, Account, Amount, Step (number/total + step name), Approver, and the Waiting date (shown in red when overdue = delay).
  • The search box instantly filters by document no, flow, type, account, and approver.
  • The column settings panel changes visibility, order, title, width, and alignment; your preferences are saved per user.

Approving / Rejecting

Clicking a row opens the document detail and the approval process timeline (each step's status, approver, entry/exit time, and note). Using the buttons at the bottom:

  • Approve: You may add an optional note.
  • Reject: A reason is required (it cannot be submitted if left empty).
  • Multiple branches: If the flow definition has additional exit branches, they appear as extra colored buttons next to Approve/Reject and route the process to the relevant branch.

Note: There is no bulk approval — each action is performed on a single document. When you approve, the next step in the flow is activated; once the document passes all steps it becomes "Approved", and if rejected it becomes "Rejected".

Tips

  • You can also perform the same approval via the quick approve/reject links in the email/WhatsApp notification sent to you.
  • Track your pending count from the "Pending Approvals" card on the Home dashboard.
