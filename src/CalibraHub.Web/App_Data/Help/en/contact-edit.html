The Account Edit screen lets you manage all information of an account (customer/supplier) in one place. Section links are on the left, the fields of the selected section on the right, and a fixed Back / Save ribbon at the top (with Delete on an existing card).

Basic Information

  • Account Code and Name (required).
  • Account Type: Customer, Supplier or Both.
  • Tax Office, Tax Number (corporate, 10 digits) or National ID Number (individual, 11 digits).
  • Sales Representative and Status (active/inactive).

Contact Information

Contact person, phone, mobile phone (WhatsApp), e-mail and website.

Address Information

Country, city, district, postal code, neighborhood and full address.

Delivery Addresses

You can define multiple delivery addresses for an account and mark one as the default. These addresses are selected on shipping documents.

Contact People

Multiple people linked to the account (title, full name, phone, e-mail, note). One is marked as the primary contact.

Stock Mappings

Mapping between the account's own stock codes and your material cards. Used for code conversion in integrations and order entry.

Account Transactions

The statement of document transactions for this account. Filtered by document type and date range; the balance is calculated from these transactions.

Groups

5-level hierarchical account grouping (classification for reporting and filtering).

Additional Fields

Custom fields defined for the account form in "Field Management" are added automatically as separate section(s).

Saving

  • Save at the top saves all sections together.
  • Back returns to the list without saving.
  • Delete appears on an existing account; a centered confirmation dialog is shown before deleting.
  • If required fields (Code, Name) are empty, saving is blocked; the tax number is validated as 10 digits and the national ID as 11 digits.

Note: The change history can be viewed in the "Change History" section of the related record.
