The Shift Definitions screen defines production shifts (e.g. Morning 08:00–16:00, Evening 16:00–24:00). Shifts are used in personnel planning and time/capacity calculations.

Cards and Actions

  • The card shows the shift name and start/end times.
  • New / Edit: Edits the shift hours and breaks.
  • Delete: Removes the shift.

Note: Assigning shifts to personnel is done from the "Shift Assignments" screen.
