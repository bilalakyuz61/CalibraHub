The Transfer Queue screen lets you monitor records (accounts, stock, documents) that are waiting to be transferred by the integration or have already been transferred.

What Is This Screen For?

  • Each row shows a transfer item: record, direction, status (pending/transferred/failed) and date.
  • You can see the failed items and trigger a re-transfer.

Note: The queue is processed automatically according to the trigger rule; here you monitor the status and intervene manually if needed.
