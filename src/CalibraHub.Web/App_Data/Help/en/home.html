The Home Dashboard is a customizable dashboard that gathers key summaries of your business on a single screen. You can arrange cards (widgets) such as pending approvals, recent documents, exchange rates, work orders, shortcuts and the calendar however you like.

How Do I Return to the Dashboard?

You can return to the Home page at any time by clicking the logo in the top left or with the Alt + H shortcut.

Cards (Widgets)

  • Welcome: User, company and live date/time.
  • Shortcuts: Quick-access tiles for your favorite screens.
  • Pending Approvals: Number of documents awaiting your approval → click to open the approval screen.
  • Exchange Rates: USD/EUR/GBP buy-sell rates and daily change direction.
  • Recent Documents: Recently processed documents; click a row to open the document.
  • Work Orders Summary: Counters by status (Planned/In Progress/Completed).
  • Sales Quotes: Draft/Pending Approval/Approved + open quote amount.
  • Stock Alerts: Stock items that have fallen below the minimum level.
  • Calendar: Monthly/weekly view; add personal events.

Note: Cards appear according to your permissions — the card of a module you cannot access will not show on the dashboard.

Customizing the Dashboard

Enter edit mode with Edit in the top right:

  • Add Widget: Add a new card from the catalog.
  • Move: Drag and drop using the handle in the card's top-left corner.
  • Resize: Resize from the card's bottom-right corner.
  • Remove: Remove with the ✕ in the card's corner (confirmation required).
  • Pages: You can add and name multiple dashboard pages (defaults: "General" and "Calendar").
  • Reset: Restores the default layout.

Use the gear icon in each card's corner for card-specific settings (e.g. the currencies shown on the exchange-rate card, the record count on the recent documents card). All changes are saved automatically and are specific to you.
