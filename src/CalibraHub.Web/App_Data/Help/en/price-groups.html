The Price List (Price Groups) screen defines price groups for different customer segments. Each group maintains a separate price list for materials.

What Is a Price Group?

A price group (e.g. "Retail", "Dealer", "General List") covers a set of material prices. The price group assigned on the account card ensures the line price is filled in automatically during document entry.

Cards and Actions

  • The card shows the group name and whether it is the default.
  • New / Edit: Edits the group and its prices.
  • Delete: Removes the group.

Note: If a group is marked as the "General List" (default), accounts without a specific price group are priced from this list.
